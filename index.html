XAR

Defense Stock Analysis

What drives the SPDR S&P Aerospace & Defense ETF, and can we project it?

Overview XAR Watch Drivers Strength Index Projections Findings

Objective

Identify what drives XAR's price, build projections with several methods (including a news-based strength index), and test whether they beat simple benchmarks. This mirrors the copper price-driver project. The difference is that XAR is a basket of companies, so market-wide moves have to be separated from sector-specific drivers.

Why XAR

  • One series for the sector: works like "the copper price."
  • Equal-weighted: mid- and small-caps count as much as the large primes (LMT, RTX, NOC, GD).
  • Aerospace exposure: commercial aerospace suppliers mean air-travel demand matters too.

Methods

  1. Monte Carlo (baseline): simulated paths from historical return and volatility.
  2. Monte Carlo (index-adjusted): expected return shifted by the strength index.
  3. Factor regression: XAR returns on market return plus GPR, the index and contract dollars.
  4. Event study: XAR returns around conflict outbreaks and NDAA signings, compared with what the market predicts.
  5. Benchmark: analyst consensus targets for the top holdings.

Phases

1Setup

Pull XAR & S&P 500 history, download GPR, build logs.

2Drivers

Market-factor regression, then add drivers one at a time.

3Index

Fix scoring rules, score past articles, validate against GPR.

4Projections

Monte Carlo, regression and event-study runs.

5Track

Log projections going forward.

6Evaluate

Backtest, regress results, compare with copper.

Backtest rule: use only information available on each date. Hindsight leaks in easily with news-driven stocks.

News Strength Index

Card-counting style: each article scores +1, 0 or −1, and the running total is the index. Edit data/articles.js to add articles.

+1 New conflict or escalation, budget increase, major contract win, allied spending pledge
−1 Ceasefire or peace deal, budget cut or CR freeze, program cancellation, cost overrun, production problem
0 Neutral or mixed coverage
